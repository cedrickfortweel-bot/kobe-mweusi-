How it works

Four steps. Nobody has to trust a stranger first.

1

Agree the deal

Settle the item and price with the seller on WhatsApp, Instagram or TikTok, as you do now.

2

Pay into escrow

Your payment goes to Kobe Mweusi, not to the seller. The seller sees that it is secured.

3

Seller delivers

Knowing the money is there, the seller ships or hands over the order.

4

Confirm and release

Happy with it? Confirm, and the seller is paid. Not happy? Open a dispute.

Questions

Before you pay, read this.

What is escrow?

A neutral hold. Your money sits with Kobe Mweusi, not with the seller, until the order is confirmed.

What if the seller never delivers?

Do not confirm the order. Open a dispute from the order and, if the claim stands, your money is returned.

What if the item is not as described?

Open a dispute, choose the reason and add photos. The outcome is a refund, a release to the seller, or a split.

When does the seller get paid?

After the buyer confirms delivery, or after a dispute is resolved in the seller's favour.

How do sellers get paid out?

After release, sellers withdraw their balance to mobile money from the dashboard.

Do I need to verify my identity?

Buyers do not. Sellers complete verification (ID and business details) before they can receive escrow payments.
